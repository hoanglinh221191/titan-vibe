Records of checks run on this package's titan.exe. QUICKSTART_RESULT.txt runs the examples of examples/quickstart against their EXPECTED.json (frame-report labels and molecule centroids). HELDOUT480_REPLAY.json replays the 480 held-out inputs against the reference outputs of this release (examples/heldout480/manifest.json); the 1.0.3 references are kept in manifest_v1.0.3.json, and SOURCE_AUDIT.json counts how many references changed. FIRST_FRAME5_REPLAY.json and REAL367_REPLAY.json replay the first frame and all 367 comparison frames of the five molecular systems, including the two Figure 1 frames: atom records are compared with the outputs deposited on 11 September 2026 at PDB precision, and the scientific report fields with REAL367_FIELDS_v1.1.0.csv; fields_changed_from_1_0_3 counts the report fields that differ from the deposited 1.0.3 reports. These records establish implementation agreement at the stated precision, not independent physical truth.
